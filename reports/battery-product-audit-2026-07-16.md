# Battery-powered product audit — 2026-07-16

## Scope and result

- Audited **67** sale-ready battery-powered SKUs in catalog.db, local Medusa, and the storefront.
- All 67 exist in Medusa, return HTTP 200, show their SKU/title, and are marked in stock.
- Battery/charger badges render consistently for all 67 PDPs.
- Result: **13 PASS**, **34 REVIEW**, **20 BLOCK**.
- No catalog or Medusa data was changed by this audit.
- The INGCO Moldova endpoint returned HTTP 403 during the run. Manufacturer confirmation therefore remains pending where local official product-image evidence is insufficient. No blocked lookup was treated as a pass.

## Production blockers

- Wrong family propagation: impact wrench, drill, S12, and rotary-hammer variants inherit specifications from unrelated/reference variants.
- Direct customer-facing contradictions include `Tensiune: 1/2″`, 1280 N·m products showing 780 N·m, 22/26/28 mm rotary hammers showing 16 mm, and `20 TEST99`.
- Several product identities/titles conflict with the official source image.
- AA alkaline batteries are described as rechargeable accumulators on two laser levels.
